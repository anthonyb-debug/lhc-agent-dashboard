What the terms mean

VOB
Verification of benefits: an insurance check submitted for a prospective patient. Counted separately for calls and chats.
VOB rate
VOBs from calls divided by inbound calls answered. 4.00% is the passing rate.
Admit
A prospective patient who was admitted. Credited to the call or chat that started it.
AHT
Average handle time per inbound call (hours:minutes:seconds).
Transfers
Calls handed off to another team member or department.
Needs coaching
Below 4.00% VOB rate for both the selected month and the selected quarter.
Streak
Consecutive months (or quarters) below 4.00%, counted back from the period selected.
